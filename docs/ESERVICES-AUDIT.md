# APSR e-services audit

Reviewed 4 November 2026 against the live Arabic website and web search results.

## Main gap

The demo collapsed six regulatory transactions into one generic licensing enquiry card. Tariffs, publications and general enquiries occupied equal positions in a catalogue that did not describe the authority’s actual approval services. Several information pages made the assistant their primary action instead of offering a service-specific next step.

The directory now separates regulatory applications, complaints and follow-up, and reference/contact information. All six regulatory services have bilingual guides, preparation summaries, process steps and official source links. Five guides link to the application entry points reached from APSR’s own buttons. Appropriate Person Criteria links to its published guide because its application landing page displayed placeholder text during review. No official application was submitted.

## Search cross-checks

- Gov.om’s Article 106 service confirms prior written approval for security over assets or liabilities: https://gov.om/en/w/request-the-authority-s-approval-106-
- Gov.om describes the Hasil complaint workflow and provider-first escalation. Its news item is dated February 2025; it is supporting context, not proof that an older portal URL is the current application route: https://stg-portal.service.gov.om/w/hasil-platform-to-help-customers-submit-complaints-of-public-services?entity=666780
- APSR’s legacy FAQ still lists older electricity suppliers. Those names were not imported into the new directory: https://www.apsr.om/en/faq
- Gov.om’s entity page includes unrelated highlighted services. They were not treated as APSR services. The page corroborates the general enquiries email and telephone: https://gov.om/en/authority-for-public-services-regulation
- The 2008 Appropriate Person Criteria document and 2009 exemption guidance appear in search results. The current live service guides were preferred over copying old application requirements.

## Limits and implementation

The regulatory services are sourced information pages with external application links, not new local transactions. The existing complaint submission, tracking and staff workflow remain a demonstration and are not connected to APSR. The tariff entry now leads to the electricity tariff tables; general enquiries displays APSR contact channels. The service assistant remains available globally.
